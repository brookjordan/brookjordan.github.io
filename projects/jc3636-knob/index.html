jc3636-knob

Small games for a GUITION JC3636K718: an ESP32-S3 with a round 360×360 touch screen, a detent knob, a haptic motor, a speaker and a ring of twelve LEDs. Most of them were written by a local language model driven by the pi agent, on a laptop, with a Claude session as coach.

How they are made

A game starts as a one-paragraph brief. The model builds it from a template that already wraps every chip, tests it in a simulator on the Mac, and has to pass a check: the simulator runs clean, the game's own feature scripts pass, and the firmware builds for the board.

The models

Occamy built the early games. Since 27 November 2026 the builder is Inkling 2 Mini, served on demand on the same laptop.
