Twenty-one screens, graded

2026-11-28 21:55 (+0800) · part 54 of 57 labdesignscreenshotsgames

Forget the briefs for one post. If you only saw the screenshots, how good is this batch?

The house style holds

Dark navy background, a cyan title at the top, white numbers, one accent colour per app. Twenty-one apps from a small model in under two hours and they look like a set. None has the smeared, half-erased text the earlier games fought with: the simulator's overdraw warnings caught every case before it shipped.

My grades

Grade Apps Why
A Sound Board, Scale Lock, Pomodoro Dial clear at a glance, use the circle, colour means something
B Key Speed, Coin Flip, Safe Heat, Morse Trainer, Memory Dial, Tea Steeper, Arpeggiator clean and readable, but a text column in a circle
C Chord Mirror, Drone Pad, Octave Jumper, Compass Spinner, Bubble Level, Orbit Dodge mostly text, or the main object is two dots
D Velocity Meter, Asteroid Cannon, Starfield, Rhythm Tap, Colour Match mostly empty, or the one thing on screen is clipped

The faults the warnings missed

  • Arpeggiator's hint line, knob: speed long-press: pattern, is cut off at both ends by the round edge ("rnob", "patterr").
  • Scale Lock's title sits under the C note dot, and the screen literally says "tap: no-op". A debug label made it to the home screen.
  • Colour Match's title has a magenta hue dot sitting on it.
  • Pomodoro's hint runs through the ring of dots.
  • Key Speed's 12-key strip slides under the target note's circle.
  • Starfield draws about six stars. Six! Calm, certainly.

Each is a one-number fix. They slip through because the warnings check text over text and text off the screen, and these are text under a shape, or a word cut at the rim by a few pixels.

The pick of the night

Sound Board. Six fat coloured pads, labels you can read from across the room, a ring on the one you pressed, volume at the bottom. Not the eight-sounds-round-the-rim I asked for, but if it had been, I'd want it to look like this.
