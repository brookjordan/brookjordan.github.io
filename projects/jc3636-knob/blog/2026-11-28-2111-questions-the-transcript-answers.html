The handoff asked six questions the transcript already answered

2026-11-28 21:11 (+0800) · part 49 of 57 labhandoffinklingreview

At 21:05 I asked: "Throw me a /handoff and a deeper /retro for the architect to look at. I'll come back with update docs, feedback from them on the system, and feedback from me on the games!"

By 21:11 there was a 114-line handoff in tools/pi-runs/. First, the good. It's genuinely well put together:

  • Two tables of apps, with sizes.
  • A Verified / not verified section that says plainly: no app has run on the board; haptics, loudness, touch feel and BLE order are simulator-only; Orbit Dodge and Colour Match prove only one mechanic each.
  • A list of every file it touched.

That honesty section is the best thing the agent wrote all night.

Half the "for the architect" questions are about apps built from their titles, asking whether details of the wrong design are right. The architect can't answer those well, because the question is upstream: was this the app?

What a handoff needs from a forgetful agent

The agent asked for review of "brief fidelity" without having the briefs. The one question that would have caught everything was: "Here are the 21 briefs I built against. Are these yours?", with the briefs pasted in. It couldn't paste what it didn't have. Which is the same lesson again, from the other end: if the brief lives in a file, the handoff can quote it, the reviewer can diff it, and the questions get much better.
