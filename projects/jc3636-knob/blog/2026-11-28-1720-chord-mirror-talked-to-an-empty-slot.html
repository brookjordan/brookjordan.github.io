Chord Mirror talked to an empty slot, and the event log told on it

2026-11-28 17:20 (+0800) · part 33 of 57 labinklingmidiblesimulator

This one is a win, mostly. Praise first.

Chord Mirror listens to the SMK-37 Pro, names the chord, and sends it to the FM-1, transposed or mirrored by the knob. Its check passed. A lesser run would have moved on. Inkling went and read what the synth actually received:

400.9 ms  midi tx slot 1 dropped (slot not ready): 90 3C 64  Note On ch1 C4 (60) vel 100
432.2 ms  midi tx slot 1 dropped (slot not ready): 90 40 64  Note On ch1 E4 (64) vel 100

Every note to the synth, dropped. The script had connected slot 0 (the keyboard) and never slot 1 (the FM-1). The app was fine; the test had no synth plugged in.

One extra line, 320 midi_conn 1 2, and the log turned green:

400.9 ms  midi tx slot 1: 90 3C 64  Note On ch1 C4 (60) vel 100  -> FM-1_BLE

and the transpose feature showed C→D, E→F#, G→A. Glorious. That is the right evidence: not a screenshot of a number, the bytes on the wire.

The part the retro got wrong

The retro calls this a gap in the simulator skill: "A one-line note in the skill would have saved one iteration per BLE app." The knob-ble-midi skill's own example script is:

200 midi_conn 0 2            # slot 0 ready, named SMK-37 Pro_BLE
250 midi_conn 1 2 fm1        # slot 1 ready as FM-1_BLE

Both slots, two lines, with comments. The note existed. It was read at 17:04 and forgotten by 17:20. And it cost one iteration in total, not one per app: every BLE app after Chord Mirror connects both slots.

And the bits the screenshots show

The brief asked for the chord name. C, E and G held shows "C". Not "C major", not "C". And the mirror-mode shot still says TRANSPOSE MODE at the top. The transpose works; the mirror is unproven.
