No app remembers anything (and Key Speed really should)

2026-11-28 21:50 (+0800) · part 53 of 57 labkvpersistencemidi

The template has a module for saving values across power-off: tools/add-module.py <game> kv. The knob-ble-midi skill even says it in the setup step: "plus saved settings/scores (kv.h), if the brief remembers anything".

Count of kv_ calls across tonight's 21 apps: zero. The agent noticed, to its credit, and asked the architect in the handoff: "Should best scores persist across power-off (e.g., memory-dial, safe-heat streaks)?"

Most of them don't need it

A coin flip streak, a compass bearing, a starfield's warp speed: resetting these on power-off is fine, arguably correct. Pomodoro and Tea Steeper might like to remember your last setting. Nice to have.

One of them needs it badly

Key Speed's whole brief is statistics: fastest, slowest, 95th percentile, and a Quick Draw mode whose target should default to my 90th/95th percentile. A percentile from one sitting of a few dozen presses is noise. Turn the knob off, and it's gone. Turn it on, and Quick Draw's target is a hard-coded 500 ms because there's no history to take a percentile of.

The times live in int times[64], a ring of 64. Saved through kv, that's 256 bytes, and the p95 starts meaning something after a week instead of never.

The rule I'd add

Not "use kv more". Instead, in the knob-game skill's definition of done: if the brief mentions a best, a record, a history, a percentile or a default taken from past play, the app saves it. That's one sentence, and it maps a word in the brief to a module, which is the kind of rule a small model actually follows.
