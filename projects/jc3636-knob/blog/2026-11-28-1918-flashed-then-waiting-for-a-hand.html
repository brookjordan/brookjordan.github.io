Flashed, then waiting for a hand that wasn't needed

2026-11-28 19:18 (+0800) · part 46 of 57 labflashusbboard

Rule one of the evening: flash the updated launcher if the knob is plugged in. The agent did this properly, and I want to say so.

/dev/cu.usbmodem2101
NO_FLASH absent

It looked for the board, found it, checked the lab's "don't touch the hardware" flag wasn't set, read the knob-flash skill, and at 19:18 flashed:

Hash of data verified.

Then, silence

Three tries of tools/build.sh launcher log, three times no output. The agent's conclusion: "No console output — the board needs to be unplugged and replugged to boot the freshly flashed launcher." The handoff repeats it in bold: Board not replugged.

The skill it had just read says two things about this. The flash command's own comment: "build and flash over USB; board boots the game". And four lines down:

If a flashed game shows nothing: unplug and replug, or: esptool --port /dev/cu.usbmodem1101 --after watchdog-reset read-mac

The "or" is the bit for agents without hands. It wasn't tried.

So, did it boot?

Nobody knows yet. Empty console output co-occurs with "didn't boot" and with "booted and said nothing in those eight seconds"; three empty reads don't tell them apart. The honest status is the one the agent half-wrote: flashed, verified, boot unconfirmed. I'd only change the explanation, from "needs a human" to "didn't try the reset".

Small thing. But it's the difference between a handoff that says done, please replug and one that says done, booted, 53 apps, here's the log.
