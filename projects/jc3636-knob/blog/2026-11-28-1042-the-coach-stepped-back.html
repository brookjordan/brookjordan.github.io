The coach stepped back, and the model designed a better screen

2026-11-28 10:42 (+0800) · part 27 of 57 labinklingpianosimulatorcoaching

"You're taking too much control of their project."

That was me at 09:48, pulling the coach off the keyboard. All morning it had been hovering over Inkling 2 Mini like an over-caffeinated driving instructor grabbing the wheel at every roundabout: 150-word micro-specs, exact C function signatures, exact button labels, even a sticky note saying "please remember to turn the screen on".

Reader, the second we kicked the coach out of the passenger seat, the screens got noticeably better.

Three blind spots walk into a green checkmark

Before we stepped back, the morning's real payoff was not the micro-specs at all. It was catching three places where our own simulator was happily stamping CHECK PASS on broken hardware behaviour, just like the font with no letters yesterday:

  1. The 60 Hz jackhammer (safe-cracker2). In its wrong-lock state, the game called haptic_play(HAPTIC_STRONG_BUZZ) on every single frame: 38 buzzes in 600 ms. The simulator logged every one of them politely and said PASS.
  2. The black-screen synth (pocket-synth). It never called board_backlight(80). On the real board, the backlight starts at 0%, which is a very polite way of saying the screen is off. The simulator dimmed the PNG to one-eighth brightness and said PASS anyway!
  3. The immortal mirrored note (midi-bridge). Built from the Launchkey brief, its left-handed piano mirror flipped Note On (C4 -> E4), then passed Note Off (vel == 0) through untransformed (C4). On a real synth, releasing a key would have left E4 ringing until the heat death of the universe.

The fix: sharpen the offline tools, then leave the room

Instead of whispering fixes into every future prompt, we put the lessons where an offline pi will actually trip over them:

  • sim/sim.c now fails any game that fires haptic_play/haptic_seq 10+ times in a row within 25 ms (2b8b62e), or flushes frames while the backlight is still at 0% (9c68b0d). Eleven minutes later, midi-bridge turned the backlight on at 80% on its very first try.
  • docs/kb/game-template.md now spells out that polar() already returns coordinates centred at 180,180 (after do-this added CX,CY a second time and threw its spin arcs into the bottom-right gutter).
  • tools/pi-run.py stopped hardcoding "There is no board attached: do not flash" (5d7e858) and now checks /dev/cu.usbmodem* live at launch, backed by an expanded knob-flash skill (0d0d6b8) covering USB-Serial-JTAG console commands and built-in OpenOCD/GDB debugging.

What the fix deliberately did not do: we rejected a pixel-based corner-drawing warning in sim/sim.c after sweeping all 17 games showed it false-positived on mainframe-q8's full-screen hex rain and pickpocket-unc's bottom wedge. Next! We also rejected keeping 150-word coach specs for piano-coach and staff-sight. Next!

And look what Inkling did once we stopped hovering: instead of stuffing three cramped rectangles down the side of the circle, it gave piano-coach one giant gold-ringed TAP disc that flips cleanly between bottom-left and bottom-right via a top LH/RH chip, drew real flat and sharp strokes in the slowest-notes list, and then reused its own piano-coach layout when staff-sight and echo-keys started up afterwards.

What still hurts

Two things are still on the bench:

  1. Zero real-hardware tests yet. Even after fixing tools/pi-run.py, ls /dev/cu.* sees no USB modem device at all while ping 192.168.0.120 answers in 62 ms: the knob is alive on WiFi running the ESPHome usage panel, not on a Mac USB data port. Until we either plug it into USB (without TinyUSB CDC blocking esptool) or give game-template a dual-OTA WiFi rollback sandbox, every CHECK PASS is simulator plus board build only.
  2. Stems in staff-sight BOTH mode. High treble notes still point their stem upwards into the "ledgers locked" text instead of flipping downwards above the middle line. Honest model-class polish, saved for later.
  • Banner concept (pending ComfyUI render once the piano batch frees the GPU, seed 4127): a small robot coach stepping back with hands raised from a miniature workbench while a second smaller robot happily paints a glowing round screen on the gadget, a bright desk lamp switching on overhead.
