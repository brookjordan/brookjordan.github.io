The left-handed piano went wireless, and the keyboard had a secret

2026-11-28 15:10 (+0800) · part 30 of 57 labmidiblepianoleft-handedota

This morning's piano apps were all simulator proofs. By three o'clock, Brook walked away from the desk holding a wireless left-handed piano: an M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro keyboard talking over Bluetooth to the JC3636K718 knob, which mirrors every note around G#4 in under a millisecond and sends it straight over Bluetooth to an M-VAVE FM-1 synth.

No USB hub. No cables. No laptop in the loop.

Measure on the Mac before touching firmware

Before writing a line of Bluetooth code on the ESP32-S3, we built a tiny native CoreMIDI probe (tools/midi-bench/midi_helper.c) and qualified both M-VAVE devices on the Mac:

  • M-VAVE FM-1 upgraded from V015 to Stock V016 (718,176 bytes via M-UPGRADE). Confirmed: 27 mini keys (F3 to G5), fixed velocity 90, GLO Voice 1 defaults to ChAll (Omni), and its physical knobs do not emit MIDI CC out.
  • M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro (already on Stock V016) revealed a surprise immediately: over USB it exposes three virtual ports (-Private, -Master, -MCP) and fires doubled notes at the exact same millisecond across -Private and -Master. Over BLE (SMK-37 Pro_BLE), it sends zero duplicates, dynamic velocity (41–114), Sustain CC64 cleanly on Channel 1, and zero Active Sensing or Clock spam.

That single bench check changed the whole architecture: instead of fighting USB Host OTG power backfeed and deduplicating doubled USB ports, we could run pure dual-Central NimBLE BLE-MIDI on the knob and walk away completely untethered.

The bug that took a disassembly to crack

Getting TEST notes out to the FM-1 took one fix (ble_store_config_init() for Just Works encryption). Getting notes in from the SMK-37 Pro was a proper mystery:

  • Both devices connected simultaneously, encrypted (ENC_CHANGE status=0), and accepted CCCD 0x0001 on handle 115 (status=0).
  • Pressing a key on the FM-1's own mini keyboard ticked up our RX counter immediately — proving the knob's BLE-MIDI receive path worked.
  • Pressing keys on the SMK-37 Pro (with its blue bt LED solid blue!) produced zero notifications.

We stopped guessing and read the disassembly of stock SMK-37_Pro_016.fwsc in amalahama/smk37-firmware-custom-mod (written for the ESP32-based Woovebox 3.0, which hit the exact same "connected, 0 MIDI notes" wall). Two missing pieces jumped straight off the page:

  1. Stale NVS bond cache: clearing the NimBLE bond store on boot (ble_store_clear()) and handling BLE_GAP_EVENT_REPEAT_PAIRING cleanly.
  2. ATT MTU exchange: macOS CoreBluetooth always negotiates MTU on connect; our NimBLE client had left it at the 23-byte default. Calling ble_att_set_preferred_mtu(247) and ble_gattc_exchange_mtu() before GATT discovery negotiated MTU 247 on the SMK-37 Pro and MTU 200 on the FM-1.

On the very next boot, notes poured through:

I (4054) ble-midi: RX slot=0 90 54 5A  (C6, vel 90)  ->  TX slot=1 90 1C 5A rc=0
I (4402) ble-midi: RX slot=0 90 53 59  (B5, vel 89)  ->  TX slot=1 90 1D 59 rc=0

Same millisecond in and out.

Stripping the UI back to what matters, then reflashing over Wi-Fi

Once it played remotely, Brook stripped away every debug control (RX, TEST, PANIC, HAND, TR) and iterated the screen live over on-demand Wi-Fi OTA (hold the screen 3 seconds → BLE stops, Wi-Fi joins Brook IoT, tools/ota-flash.sh midi-bridge 192.168.0.120 streams the new binary into ota_0/ota_1, reboots back into BLE):

  • Full 37-key keyboard (C3–C6) across the widest band of the round screen.
  • Per-note 5-colour pairing (Red, Cyan, Amber, Green, Magenta): every held finger gets a 2 px coloured outline and its mirrored sounding note lights up solid in the exact same colour — way cleaner for chords than drawing lines between keys.
  • Lockable piano symmetry axes: defaults to G#4 (68) (2 semitones from the dead centre of C3–C6, and an exact white-to-white / black-to-black mirror axis), with a Mirror + Lock button that snaps the bezel cleanly through D and G# axes (or unlocks for free semitones) and auto-hides the mirror line 2 seconds after turning.
  • Lock-free redraw fix: early playing showed occasional visually stuck red keys because redraw = false ran after the ~15 ms QSPI flush, wiping out Note Offs arriving mid-frame from nimble_host_task. Switching to a monotonic ui_gen != drawn_gen counter killed it completely.

Taught back to the offline lab

Everything we measured on the SMK-37 Pro, the FM-1, NimBLE's MTU/bond traps, and Wi-Fi OTA is now filed in .agents/skills/knob-ble-midi/SKILL.md and docs/kb/ble-midi-devices.md so the offline pi agent can build on these devices without hitting a single one of today's traps.

Meanwhile, in the background with tools/pi-runs/NO_FLASH keeping the desk knob safe, Inkling 2 Mini Q8 quietly finished six more zero-coach-note games (stop-the-sweep A+, lock-pick B+, tug-of-war B-, hot-potato A, redline A+, snail-race A+), bringing the suite to 37 built apps before we paused the model server to free 43 GB of unified memory for artwork and the Apple Watch–style home screen.
