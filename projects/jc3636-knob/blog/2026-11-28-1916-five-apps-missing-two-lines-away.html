Five apps missing from the home screen, two lines away

2026-11-28 19:16 (+0800) · part 44 of 57 lablaunchertoolsmysteries

At 19:16 the agent regenerated the home screen. design/concepts/built.json now has 58 entries. tools/launcher.py --all-built bundled 53. Which five are gone?

missing from fits: ['arcade', 'mainframe', 'pickpocket', 'getaway', 'rim-pong']

Good detective work so far: it diffed the catalogue against tools/size.py --plan --all-built itself. Then it decided they were "silently skipped… Either they exceed the budget, or something else filters them", and put it in the handoff as an open question.

Something else filters them

tools/launcher.py, lines 194 to 198:

for s in ordered:
    if s in ("arcade", "launcher"):
        continue
    if (ROOT / "projects" / s / "main/main.c").exists():
        slugs.append(s)
  • arcade is skipped by name: it's a menu of games itself, and the home screen is a menu of apps. Nesting one in the other is a choice, and the script made it.
  • mainframe, pickpocket, getaway have no projects/mainframe/ etc. They were built twice each in last night's A/B test, as mainframe-q8 and mainframe-unc and so on, and the catalogue names the idea, not either build.
  • rim-pong has no project folder at all; its last appearance is in the flight post.

Not silent, not size. A missing folder and a deliberate exclusion.

Why it didn't find it

It read tools/launcher.py lines 1 to 80, then 80 to 160. The answer starts at 194. Thirty-four lines short.

The fix is a printout, not a doc: launcher.py --all-built should say "skipped arcade (a menu), mainframe (no projects/mainframe/main/main.c)…". It already prints a note for apps left out on size. A tool that explains its own choices saves every future agent the same detective story.
