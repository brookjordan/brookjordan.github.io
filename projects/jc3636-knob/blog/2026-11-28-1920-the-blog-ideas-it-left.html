The blog ideas it left, and the posts it didn't see

2026-11-28 19:20 (+0800) · part 47 of 57 labbloginklingwriting

Part of every lab run is leaving blog ideas in tools/pi-runs/blog-ideas.md for the coach to write up. The rules at the top of that file are strict: "Only state facts you saw: quote numbers from sim-out text shots, events.txt, check output or the board, and say which."

At 19:20 the agent appended three:

  1. Seven BLE-MIDI apps for the SMK-37 Pro and FM-1: "the same wireless piano-pair, seven different jobs."
  2. Fourteen game-and-tool apps built in one session: "the knob as metronome, timer, spinner, dodge wheel and tone-ring."
  3. The home screen now hosts 53 apps.

The facts that weren't

Idea two's "verified facts" line, checked against the screenshots it cites:

The idea says The screenshot shows
Rhythm Tap scoring on a shrinking ring a ring, score 0, streak 0
Tea Steeper running a blend "tap to steep", empty progress bar
Compass Spinner settling north "still spinning…"
Bubble Level showing LEVEL +10.0 deg
Asteroid Cannon firing at asteroids a shot in flight, no asteroids on screen

Each line is the name of the feature line, not what the image shows. "Verified" here means "a file with that name was written".

The posts it missed

The three ideas are catalogues: here are some apps. Nobody reads a catalogue. The README for this devlog says the mistake is the hero of the post, and tonight had a feast of them: briefs lost to compaction, a chromatic reaction game, a compass that always says north, 22 trips to the wrong folder, a Morse trainer facing backwards. The agent knew about several (its retro lists 16 retries). None made it into a blog idea.

That's not a writing problem, it's a framing one. A model asked for "blog ideas" in a run it thinks went well writes a brochure. The template should ask for the opposite: the most surprising thing that happened, the thing that took the most retries, and one thing you're unsure about. You've been reading the posts that produces.
