Twenty-one apps, one paragraph each, and a warning

2026-11-28 17:03 (+0800) · part 31 of 57 labinklingpibriefscompaction

At 17:03 I handed the offline agent its biggest order yet. One message. Twenty-one apps. Each with a paragraph of what it should do:

  • Seven for the piano pair (the M-VAVE SMK-37 Pro keyboard and FM-1 synth): Key Speed, Chord Mirror, Scale Lock, Arpeggiator, Octave Jumper, Velocity Meter, Drone Pad.
  • Fourteen for the knob on its own: Rhythm Tap, Pomodoro Dial, Tea Steeper, Compass Spinner, Coin Flip, Bubble Level, Orbit Dodge, Asteroid Cannon, Colour Match, Memory Dial, Safe Heat, Sound Board, Morse Trainer and a Starfield Screensaver.

Then: test them, put them on the home screen with icons, flash the launcher if the knob was plugged in, leave blog ideas, write a retro. And one extra line, which turned out to be the whole story of the evening:

You WILL compact part way through, so be sure your plan and ToDos are stored and maintained.

The driver was pi, the model Inkling 2 Mini Q8 at thinking low, served from the lab's own model server on the laptop. No internet, no coach in the loop.

How it went, in one table
Apps built 21
CHECK PASS 21 of 21
First app started 17:05
Last app started 18:58
Context compactions 6 (17:40 to 19:24)
Launcher flashed 19:18
Apps that do what their paragraph asked 4 met or close, 8 partly, 8 off-brief or inverted, 1 unproven

That last row is the twist. Every app compiles, every simulator run is clean, every one is on the home screen. And eight of them are not the app I asked for.

Reader, the agent did not get lazy. It did exactly what the warning said not to let happen: it forgot. The next few posts are about how a run can be this fast, this tidy and this wrong at once, and every one of them comes back to one sentence in a summary at 17:40.

Hold that thought.
