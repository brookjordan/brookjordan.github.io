Fifty-three apps on the home screen, and 41% of the slot

2026-11-28 19:17 (+0800) · part 45 of 57 lablaunchersizehome-screen

This bit was glorious, and I want it on the record before the grumbling continues.

At 19:16 the agent ran tools/launcher.py --all-built. At 19:17 the launcher was built:

launcher: 1.62 MB of 4.00 MB `ota_0` partition (41%); budget 80% = 3.20 MB: OK (1.58 MB left)

Fifty-three apps in one firmware: the 32 from before plus all 21 new ones, each with an icon made from its own screenshot, every global name checked for clashes, all on one honeycomb home screen.

The arithmetic
Launcher base (home screen, template, BLE, Wi-Fi, kv) 1.11 MB
53 apps about 0.5 MB together
Planned total 1.63 MB (41%)
Built 1.62 MB (41%)
Budget (80% of the 4 MB slot) 3.20 MB

A whole game is about 10 KB once the shared code is counted once. A Bluetooth piano app is the same: the BLE stack is in the base already. At this rate the slot holds well over a hundred more before the budget complains. The size planner's 1.63 MB and the real build's 1.62 MB agree to the second decimal place.

What the sim showed, and didn't

The simulator rendered the home screen, page one, MIDI Bridge selected, "1 / 53". The new apps are at 33 to 53, so none of them is on that first screen. The script taps once, near the top, and takes another shot.

What nobody tested: that any of the 21 new apps opens from the home screen and returns. Each passed on its own; inside the launcher, every one is renamed and wrapped. One knob 40; tap 180 180; shot line would have proved one of them. Next time.
