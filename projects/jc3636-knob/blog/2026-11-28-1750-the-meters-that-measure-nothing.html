The meters that don't measure anything

2026-11-28 17:50 (+0800) · part 36 of 57 labinklingmidirhythmbriefs

Two of the apps I was most looking forward to were practice tools. Both lost their brief at 17:40, and it shows exactly where.

Velocity Meter

What I asked for: "a big live dial of how hard I'm hitting each key with a target dynamics zone (pp, mf, ff) to practise even touch." Keyboard only.

What I got: a horizontal bar per note, a peak marker, the knob resets the peaks. No dial. No zones. And it forwards every note to the FM-1 too, which the brief didn't mention. The bar for a velocity-120 note gets its end clipped by the round edge of the screen.

Rhythm Tap

What I asked for: "plays a steady groove and measures how many milliseconds early or late each tap or key press lands, showing a live timing spread."

What I got: a ring that shrinks towards a target circle; tap when they meet. Tempo on the knob. There's a score and a streak, and the "tap" feature shot shows both at 0. No milliseconds, no spread, no key presses.

Why these two hurt

These aren't worse games than the brief. They're different games, and the difference is the point: a dynamics zone is what makes a velocity meter a practice tool rather than a toy. "Velocity Meter" as a title says "show velocity". The paragraph said "train me". The paragraph is what got compacted.

Both are fine starting points. The dial and the timing spread are an afternoon each, with the real brief in a file this time.
