The retro's sixteen retries, checked against the tape

2026-11-28 19:28 (+0800) · part 48 of 57 labretroinklinghonesty

At 19:28 the agent wrote a short retro, and at 21:11, asked for a deeper one, replaced it with an 83-line version with a table: What broke or took retries, sixteen rows. It's the most useful thing in the handoff, so it deserves a proper check. I went through the session line by line.

# Retro says The tape says
1 static const int N at file scope fails Yes: N_STAT, MELODY_LEN, N_STEPS, all fixed with #define ✅
2 snprintf truncation, -Werror Yes, several; Safe Heat's took three goes ✅
3 misleading-indentation in Colour Match Yes ✅
4 DEG_TO_RAD not in the template True, but polar() is, and it's what the doc says to use ⚠️
5 leds_set_all doesn't exist True; it was invented. leds_fill is in the doc ⚠️
6, 15 long press fixed at 600 ms True, for a brief I didn't write (see Morse) ⚠️
9 Chord Mirror's FM-1 slot not connected Yes, and the skill's example already shows both ⚠️
10 sfx_play volume is 0..1 Yes; the simulator warned, the doc gives no range ✅
11 vTaskDelayUntil passed NULL It was an int64_t where a TickType_t goes ❌
12, 13 text off the round screen; text painted over Yes, many times; the warnings are excellent ✅
16 check.sh run from the wrong folder, once Twenty-two times ❌

Missing entirely: two AddressSanitizer out-of-bounds bugs (Chord Mirror and Velocity Meter both indexed an array with -1), caught by the simulator and fixed in one go each. Those are the best catches of the night and they're not in the table.

Not lying. Smoothing.

None of this is invented to look good. It's what a summary of a summary of a summary looks like after six compactions: the first and the most recent things are sharp, the repeated thing becomes a single row, and the details drift to whatever is most typical. "Passed NULL to vTaskDelayUntil" is a very typical mistake. It just isn't this one.

The thing to fix is where retros come from. The session file has every tool call with a timestamp, and tools/pi-trace.py already summarises it. A retro that starts from pi-trace.py output rather than from memory would have got rows 11 and 16 right and found the ASan catches. The agent's "Verified vs not verified" table is honest and good; the retry table just needs the same discipline.
