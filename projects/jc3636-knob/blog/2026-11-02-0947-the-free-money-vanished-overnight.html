The free money vanished overnight

2026-11-02 09:47 · part 2 of 20 usage paneldeepseekhonesty

The DeepSeek pane has one job: show how much credit is left. On the evening of 1 November it said 33.15 of granted credit. By 09:47 the next morning it said 0.00. Nobody touched anything. Well. Somebody touched something.

First, a confession about a sentence

Before any of this, the first version of the DeepSeek ticket contained a beautifully confident line: DeepSeek "does not expose usage windows at all".

It was false. Completely, cheerfully false.

The vault has a little gate called claim-check.py that grumbles at any sentence describing how a tool behaves without saying where the claim came from. It grumbled. The research said otherwise. The sentence now lives on in the ticket as a struck-out correction, which I love, because a wrong claim you can still see is a lesson, and a wrong claim you quietly deleted is just a future wrong claim.

Two shapes of money

A subscription window refills: five hours, seven days, back to full. A DeepSeek balance does not refill. It is a jar of coins. So the pane can't draw "percent of window used", because there is no window.

The fix was to give the jar a lid: the bar is min(balance / 10, 1), so ten units fills it, and anything above stays full, with the actual amount printed inside the bar as US$42.05. No headline, no "total credit" label. They crowded the round screen without telling you anything the bar didn't.

(The bar text offset took two goes. 75 % of the text height was too low. 50 % is current. I mention this because it is the most important number in the whole post and nobody will ever notice it.)

The rule: verified, or not shown

Here's the bit I'm proudest of. The pane could have shown an expiry date for the granted credit. There was even one, typed in by hand: 2026-11-20. There was an environment variable fallback for it. There was a little config file for it.

All three got deleted, under one rule: the pane shows verified data only, nothing assumed. The API key gives total_balance, granted_balance and topped_up_balance. It does not give an expiry. So the pane does not show an expiry. If the number isn't coming from DeepSeek, it isn't going on my desk.

And then the money vanished

Next morning: granted balance 0.00, topped-up 9.46, total 9.46. The agent's cached payload and a direct call to /user/balance agreed.

My first instinct was a dramatic theory about expiring grants. The boring truth, which the ticket records with the sort of dignity I can only aspire to, is that the granted credit had simply been used. By me. On other things. The expiry question closed "for the wrong reason", which is the funniest resolution line in the whole vault.

So the pane worked perfectly. It reported, accurately, that I had spent my free money. Thank you, pane. Very helpful. Ten out of ten. Would be told off by a desk ornament again.
