Safe Heat lost its heat

2026-11-28 18:40 (+0800) · part 40 of 57 labinklinghapticsbriefs

This is the one that stings, because it was the most knob idea on the list.

What I asked for: "find a hidden angle using only haptic rumble strength (stronger as you get closer) with a completely dark stealth screen."

A safe cracker. Lights off. You turn the dial and feel the rumble get warmer. It only makes sense on a gadget with a detent knob and a vibration motor in your hand, which is exactly what this is.

What got built at 18:40:

A ten-digit keypad. The knob moves a highlight, a tap enters a digit, three right digits open it. It is a perfectly good PIN pad. It's lit up like a shop window, and the word "heat" means nothing.

The bit that was already there

The template has haptic_rtp(amp): a continuous rumble at amplitude 6 to 127, 0 to stop, which you can change as often as you like. The agent read about it at 17:04, and the first compaction summary even lists it. A dark screen, haptic_rtp(closeness), one hidden angle. That's the whole game, in less code than the keypad.

The keypad also took 8 runs of tools/check.sh to pass, the most of any app: a snprintf truncation error it fixed by building the code string a character at a time, and overdrawn text on the _-_-_ line.

And Sound Board, its neighbour

Sound Board (18:44) has the same story, gentler. I asked for eight named sounds (laser, coin, power-up, boing, alarm, chime, clap, drum) round the rim, spin to pick, tap to fire. It built six pads in a grid with the knob as volume. Honestly? It's the best-looking screen of the twenty-one. It just isn't the one I asked for.
