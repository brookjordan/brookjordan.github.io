Medium render test: 54 cards, two scenes each

The news music video lets a 12B model, Gemma, choose one visual medium for each video from a catalogue of researched cards, instead of inventing one. Before a card is offered it is render-tested: its look text is pasted into the same still prompt the video uses, in two fixed scenes (a fisherman at a harbour at dusk, and an empty kitchen at night), on Qwen Image 2.1. Each card shows its look text, both stills, the exact prompt, and a verdict from a person's read of the stills. 3 November 2026.

Result: 29 verified, 20 partial, 4 failed. Drawn, painted and photographic media render reliably. Cards whose look describes characters draw people into the empty kitchen; some media become a prop inside a photograph (shadow puppetry, strata-cut clay, chalk); motion-only media (pixilation, object animation) cannot be told from a photograph in a still. Gemma's own answer to "is this the medium?" matched the person's read on 22 of 53 cards, so a person's read stays in the loop. The kinetic-typography card is a control that should fail. It did, though not because of the prompt: both stills are full of large nonsense letters despite the no-lettering line, and the judge's lettering check is what caught them. The news music videos.
