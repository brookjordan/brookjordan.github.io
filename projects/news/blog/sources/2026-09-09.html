Home AI server build

I sketched a longer-term replacement for the 8 GB M1 Mac mini, aimed at running quantised Qwen models locally. A small 16 GB RTX 2000 Ada box would be quiet and efficient; a used 24 GB RTX 3090 is more likely to hold the workload in VRAM but brings a much larger case, more heat and a serious PSU question. The marketplace claims still need checking.

The strongest lead was a roughly S$950 RTX 3090, possibly paired with a Ryzen 5 3600 host. I captured the uncertain hardware details in the dated GPU and host-market evidence packet rather than pretending they were verified.
