PCB-058

PCB-058 Build the alias and near links

Goal

Brook's design: the style system begins with one entry per idea, and repeats and near-synonyms point at it with @see. alias means the same thing. near means close but different, such as a zoom against a dolly, and those form the list to fill in later.

Background and measurements: the trials note.

2026-11-03 11:34: first full run finished

All 826 batches over Raw and the 1,129 Round 1 harvests were sent, for $0.51 notional, with no unparsable replies. see_near.json holds 1,738 links, 340 alias and 1,398 near, plus 24 oversized groups for review. Of 20 random links read by one reader, about 13 were sound, including cross-language ones such as 望遠レンズを多用 @see Telephoto lens, and about 6 were stretches, such as "Faux Hand held @see faux documentary". One difference looked wrong to the reader: it calls plano próximo tighter than primer plano, the reverse of the usual Spanish scale. So treat near links as candidates, not settled, and treat the differences as unchecked.

2026-11-03: batching rebuilt

`see_near.py` now builds batches with no tokens. Each name, its aliases and its distinction become a TF-IDF vector with name words weighted three times and name trigrams added. Each name keeps up to 12 neighbours above 0.18 similarity across all dimensions, and batches of 80 grow outwards along those links. Names with no neighbour, 618 of 7,979, are not sent. Links are flattened so each points at a main that is not itself linked. Progress is saved in `see_near.progress.json`; `--plan` shows batches and a pair check with no tokens, and `--max-cost` stops the run. The 46 links from 2026-11-02 are kept in `Sweep 2/see_near.2026-11-02.json`, since `see_near.json` is now rewritten by each run.

Measured against the 46 links from the alphabetical test: 24 pairs are now neighbours and 21 share a batch. Most misses are translations or synonyms with no shared words, such as dettaglio against extreme close-up and Amerikanische Einstellung against cowboy shot. That eval set favours alphabetically close pairs, so it understates the new method's reach to pairs the old one could not see, which has not been measured. Five test batches cost $0.026 notional, about $0.005 a batch, for 84 links in the last three. Most read as sensible; some near links are stretches, such as "Slow pan shot @see Reveal pan shot", and one rested on a junk row.

2026-11-03 17:00: final pass over all of Round 1

see_near.py over Raw and the 2,880 Round 1 harvests: 13,245 names, 12,501 with a neighbour, 1,317 batches. see_near.json now holds 3,723 links, 549 alias and 3,174 near, with 81 oversized groups for review, for $1.57 notional across all three runs. see_rows.py: 16,099 rows, 12,313 distinct after exact and listed-alias matching, so 3,786 rows (24%) become @see; most repeated are establishing shot (56), cold open (41) and long take (35).
