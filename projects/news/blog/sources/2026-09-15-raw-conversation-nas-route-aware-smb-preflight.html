A synthesized evidence packet already exists for the NAS/ComfyUI investigation. This packet preserves the route-specific SMB clarification as raw conversation context.

User: Verify the NAS SMB share with smbutil view. Note that this could be over the 10.10.10, the WAN, or the tailscale connection, right?

Assistant: Yes. smbutil view is transport-agnostic: it uses the route selected for the specific hostname or IP. The command alone does not tell us whether the connection used LAN, WAN, or Tailscale.

The observed routes were:

  • 10.10.10.2 via en0: local/direct LAN.
  • 192.168.0.224 via en0: local LAN.
  • 100.84.158.31 via utun4: Tailscale.

smbutil view //100.84.158.31 succeeded and listed model-store and model-cache. That proves SMB discovery over Tailscale, not authenticated access or a mounted share usable by ComfyUI.

The preflight should test each candidate address independently and record the address, selected interface, route class, TCP/445 result, SMB discovery, authentication state, mount state, and ComfyUI model registration. The test belongs on the MacBook, because ComfyUI runs there; n8n on the NAS cannot infer the MacBook's interface state without querying it.
