PCB-013 Probe 10-second renders at 1280x704 across frozen cases

Question

Can the production podcast transition graph render 10-second clips (250 frames @ 25 fps) for every frozen PCB-012 case, and what does the duration/memory curve look like on the 36 GB MPS machine?

Why

The production builder only renders 49-frame (~2 s) clips. PCB-012 wants longer recipes validated against real episode prompts before any production change. Case C (exec 2572, v2026.38.4-18.29) proved a 10 s render is achievable and its video is the upgrade candidate Brook requested for that episode.

Method

  • Production transition graph unchanged (one-stage Q3_K_M distilled, 1280×704, FLF2V guides at frame 0/249 strength 1.0, euler_ancestral + manual 9-sigma schedule, DualCFG 1.0, tiled VAE decode, 25 fps).
  • Each case uses its real recorded motion prompt (from Prepare video render per execution) and its verified cover pair.
  • Seeds follow the production derivation: abs(exe * 2654435761) % 2^32.
  • One render at a time; results logged to the local vault/tmp/pcb012/batch10s_results.jsonl.

State (2026-09-19)

  • Case C (2572): ✅ 9.96 s / 249 frames, wall 2196 s, endpoints 4.2/3.3 /255. Delivered to Brook (/tmp/pcb012-caseC-10s.mp4).
  • Case A (2577): rendering.
  • Cases B (2575), D (2560), E (2506): queued behind A via sequential batch.
  • Case E pair is the recovered 2506/2503 published pair (hash-verified against NAS v2026.38.2-08.49).

Memory envelope evidence

See the duration-curve probe packet: 250f fails under the default MPS watermark (0.8 → 22.46 GiB cap) deterministically, succeeds with PYTORCH_MPS_HIGH_WATERMARK_RATIO=0.0 (uncapped server, plateau 7.6–9.4 GiB server-free during sampling).

Acceptance criteria

  • Five 10 s renders attempted (C done, A/B/D/E remaining), failures recorded with OOM evidence.
  • Wall time, seed, frame count, endpoint fidelity and visual verdict recorded per case.
  • Duration/memory curve summarised and folded back into the duration boundary ticket.
  • No production workflow change.
