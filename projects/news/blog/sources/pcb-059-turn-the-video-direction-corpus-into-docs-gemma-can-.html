PCB-059

PCB-059 Turn the video direction corpus into docs Gemma can choose from

Goal

Brook, 2026-11-03: use the video direction research to make docs Gemma can use in the podcast's music video, starting from how the song-style data was reshaped for PCB-047 but tailored to this data.

The plan is the plan for video direction docs: one small catalogue per slot of the direction (medium, world look, opening and climax devices, shot moves), canonical entries built from the @see links, cards labelled by a local model panel, render-tested before they are offered, then chosen by enum in direction.ts.

To do

  • First slot and review depth decided, 2026-11-03 (below).
  • Round 1 complete, 2,880 of 2,880 harvests, 2026-11-03 16:18. Final see_near.py pass running.
  • Canonical entries: Sweep 2/build_entries.py writes entries.json (first build, rerun after the final linking pass).
  • Label slots and draft cards with the local panel; reconcile. Running: Sweep 2/label_entries.py, Gemma then Qwen then Phi, output in Sweep 2/Docs build/.
  • Generate families and cards into podcastbuilder/data/video/; Brook reads a sample.
  • Render-test an approved shortlist of cards.
  • Wire the choice into direction.ts with logged reasons and one random shortlist slot.
  • Compare against free-text direction on at least three episodes.

Completion criterion

A medium choice in the live shots stage, from cards that passed a render test, whose stills pass the judge at least as often as free-text direction and which Brook prefers on at least three episodes.

Decisions, 2026-11-03

  • Medium first, then world look. Brook left it to the agent's judgement and asked that the choice not rest on resemblance to the song-style selector. Reasons: medium is free text today and sets the look of every still and clip; PCB-050 traced failed stills to looks that did not fit their shots; and the corpus is richest there, with about 3,150 aesthetic rows plus animation-technique, material and capture-medium rows.
  • Review depth. Every card is checked by script and render test; Brook reads about five cards per family before that family is offered to Gemma.
  • GPU. Brook: no podcasts are scheduled, so render qualification may use the GPU freely.

2026-11-03 16:30: entries built, panel labelling started

  • build_entries.py reads tables by header, so the 487 older ten-column Raw tables keep their Suits and Veto columns, which map onto a card's use-when and against. 16,151 rows became 12,849 entries; 1,458 are named in more than one harvest; 2,197 carry a flag, mostly "no sourced row" (1,894).
  • A first build joined entries on the aliases rows list, and chains merged unrelated ideas: establishing shot absorbed American shot and close shot, and deep focus absorbed shallow focus. Entries now merge only on exact names and see_near.py alias links, which point one level deep; listed aliases are kept as other names only. A remaining error of that kind: "L-cut" lists J-cut among its other names.
  • Medium and look candidates: 3,768 unflagged entries in the aesthetic, animation-technique, material or capture-medium dimensions. A two-batch Gemma pilot labelled stop-motion, claymation, cut-out, pixilation and 16mm medium, chiaroscuro and low-key lighting look, and dolly zoom, long take and Dutch angle neither, at about 10 s a batch of ten. It also called kinetic typography a medium, which the no-lettering rule should fail at the render test, and left stop-motion and stop-motion animation as two entries.
