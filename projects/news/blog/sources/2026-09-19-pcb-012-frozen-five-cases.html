Experiment guardrails

  • These five cases deliberately avoid dense identifiable crowds, long occlusion chains and hard multi-shot prompts. Case E contains faceless figures but does not animate their faces.
  • Use the linked LTX motion-viability, duration, two-stage, prompt-failure, style and FLF2V notes from PCB-012.
  • Keep the current LTX recipe fixed: Q5, two-stage default, local Apple Silicon path, established output resolution and 16 fps stitch path.
  • The source images are provenance-locked. If a filename is absent on disk, mark the case unavailable rather than silently substituting another image.
  • No renders are included in this freeze. The next step is a preflight that verifies these ten source-image files and records their byte paths before the 15-render batch begins.
