Evicting the cloud: music and video generation moves onto the Mac

Music and video had been two remote assumptions wearing one coat. Music needed a local YuE2 path with a stable contract; video needed native LTX-2.5 in its own isolated ComfyUI. We stopped pretending it was one problem.

Written retrospectively from records covering 16 September 2026.

The podcast builder was carrying two different remote-generation assumptions, and we finally stopped pretending they were one problem. Music needed a local YuE2 path that could return a WAV with a stable contract. Video needed native Apple Silicon LTX-2.5, with its own isolated ComfyUI profile, model registration and guide-frame handling. The cutover was therefore less "swap a model" and more "make two generators agree to hand over their work without setting fire to the rest of the graph".

What happened

The old music route was replaced by YuE2, while the former remote video path gave way to native LTX-2.5. These were separate contracts. YuE2 produced intro_2491_00001.wav, recorded as 2,303,788 bytes and 11.999 seconds. LTX ran in an isolated profile, then restored normal ComfyUI before stitching. The video side also needed LTXVCropGuides between sampling and decode, because guide padding could turn a requested 49-frame transition into 65 frames. We kept the previous builder inactive rather than deleting it, which is the engineering equivalent of leaving a fire exit unobstructed.

What the evidence changed

The first useful result was not a glossy final episode. Execution 2491 demonstrated the native transition, normal-profile restoration, VHS stitching and final-video retrieval before the parallel music branch failed. That distinction mattered: one branch can produce a real artefact while the whole workflow still fails. Later, execution 2502 exercised the saved manifest and retrieval nodes against those artefacts, reading 30 database-backed binaries totalling 9,329,116 bytes. The record supports a working hand-off between stages, not a claim that every possible storage route had become independent.

The project map records the dated cutover on 16 September and identifies workflow 2k7x4U8GgLeSFpRE as the new builder, with the former workflow retained for rollback. The ticket resolution was written on 19 September, so we keep those dates distinct: the decision was recorded later than the production event it describes.

Where we left it

The first full production run, execution 2560, completed 98/98 nodes green and published an episode. That is the recorded milestone. It does not mean spoken TTS changed too, and it does not prove that all media had become NAS-independent. Local LTX model transfer, storage reliability and a clean NAS-absent validation run remained with PCB-008. YuE2 music, spoken TTS and the earlier MiniMax lyric contract were separate stages, so the music cutover did not quietly replace the speech engine.

We kept rollback in place, because architecture diagrams are optimistic and storage outages are not. The practical result was a production cutover with a recoverable old path, not a declaration that every dependency had vanished.

That boundary kept the cutover honest: a measured hand-off, not a promise that the storage layer had stopped mattering.
