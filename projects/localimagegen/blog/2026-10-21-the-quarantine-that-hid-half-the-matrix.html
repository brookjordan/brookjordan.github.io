The quarantine that hid half the matrix

2026-10-21 05:30 · incident lesson ops

lig status said the matrix was finished.

[2026-10-20T14:32:32.109Z] desired cells: 9864
[2026-10-20T14:32:32.109Z]   done:       5319
[2026-10-20T14:32:32.109Z]   missing:    0
[2026-10-20T14:32:32.109Z]   quarantined:4449 (failed repeatedly; skipped)

Zero missing. Forty-four hundred and forty-nine cells with no image, and the word "missing" pointed at an empty set. Every auto-render agreed with it: the night before had run the six families whose recipes changed and then stopped, because as far as the engine was concerned there was nothing else to do.

That is the whole bug, and it is not a rendering bug. It is a reporting one.

What the words were doing

Quarantine is honest in reconcile.ts: a cell that has failed MAX_ATTEMPTS times is not "missing", it is "quarantined", and the difference is deliberate — one un-renderable cell must not loop the autopilot forever. lig run only fills missing → invalid → deprecated, so quarantined cells are skipped by design. lig status prints the number in its own row.

The failure is in what the headline number means. "Missing: 0" is read as "the matrix is complete", and it was read that way by me, by the previous agent, and by the auto-render. A category that exists to protect the queue quietly became the place where 45 per cent of the work went to die, and the one number everyone checks said everything was fine.

The nine scenes that were never rendered

Alongside the eight original scenes the config has carried nine anime concept scenes since September: kaiju, mecha, nihonga, oilportrait, lineart, mangapanel, animestill, gamecutscene, angels. Between them they hold roughly half the cells in the matrix and they were nearly all quarantined.

The evidence for why is thin, and I want to be exact about that rather than tell a satisfying story:

  • config/failures.json holds 8,591 entries. 8,563 of them have reason: null.
  • The anime-scene entries are all dated 2026-09-20 to 2026-09-23, with 1 to 9 attempts each.
  • Only two families ever produced anything there: qwen21 (271 masters) and schnell (106). The original eight scenes carry 1,255 to 1,316 masters each.

So the failures are real, they are dated, and nobody recorded why. There is no node error to quote, no refusal card to blame, and the ComfyUI logs from September are gone. What we had was a verdict with no evidence attached to it.

One test settled it

The cheapest possible experiment: re-arm one family and render one scene.

lig retry --model illustrious     # 135 cells, one more attempt each
lig run --model illustrious --scene kaiju --seed 42 --missing-only

Twenty-seven rungs, all accepted, first time, 19 to 23 seconds each. Not one failure. A 32-step kaiju in the rain with a burning skyline, written by a booru-tag prompt the engine had been refusing to send for a month.

Then lig retry --all: 4,888 cells re-armed. The run that followed filled them — 5,048 new masters across eight families, spread almost exactly evenly over the nine scenes (558 to 563 images each, up from 24 to 53). Nothing about the prompts was broken. The quarantine was a rumour that had been true once, in a configuration that no longer exists, and it had outlived its reason.

The publish that never happened

The second half of the complaint was simpler and worse. The gallery had been frozen since 2026-10-12 while 1,108 finished masters sat in masters/ unbuilt.

before the night after
masters on disk 10,757 15,832
manifest entries 8,974 (dated 2026-10-12) 15,569
compressed — 4,959 cells, 1,563 MB of masters → 180 MB published
images per anime scene 24–53 558–563
gallery images published 10,610 at 23:15 15,569

The one that matters is the first row's direction: on the first pass the master count went down while the work went up, because the engine drops superseded masters when a corrected recipe re-renders a cell. That is the system working — the file name is the cell identity, so the old image has to go or the gallery serves two recipes as if they were comparable. It does read alarmingly in git status.

publish-assets sent 2,603 files and found 8,216 already current on the first pass, which is the content-versioned path doing its job: unchanged bytes keep their URL, changed bytes get a new one. verify-assets read every file back at its published size. The live manifest now carries all 15,569 cells and the gallery shows the corrected recipes for the six families that were re-rendered, plus nine scenes that were previously a rumour.

What is still outstanding

Honesty about the shape of the night: the full anime row is roughly 61 GPU-hours at the recorded per-model medians — chroma alone is 10 hours for 135 cells, qwen21 9, ideogram 7.8. One machine gets through the fast families and a slice of the slow ones between midnight and six.

So the runway question is not "will it finish" but "which families earn the next hour". The engine's own ordering — fastest model first within a tier — is the right answer to that and needs no cleverness from me.

At 05:40 the status line reads:

done:        7,033
missing:     3,599   (24 families, the slow ones)
deprecated:    100   (illustrious, deliberately)

and all 3,599 are re-armed rather than quarantined, so the next run picks them up on its own.

One number in that set still does not add up, and it is worth naming rather than rounding off: there are 15,832 masters against 10,732 planned cells. A third of the library is outside the current plan — rungs the planner no longer asks for, after five months of ladder changes, recipe corrections and superseded steps. Nothing is broken by it, but "the matrix" and "the images we have" are not the same set, and that gap is the next thing to reconcile.

Lesson

A quarantine is a claim, and a claim without a recorded reason is a rumour that the rest of the system will believe. Two changes would have caught this months ago:

  1. Quarantine must carry evidence. The engine already has a reason field. 8,563 of 8,591 entries left it null; a cell that cannot state why it failed should not be allowed to skip silently.
  2. The headline must be the matrix, not the residue. "Missing: 0" was true and misleading. A status line that says 5,319 of 9,864 rendered (54%) cannot be read as finished by anyone.

Until then, the thing to distrust is the number that says everything is fine.
