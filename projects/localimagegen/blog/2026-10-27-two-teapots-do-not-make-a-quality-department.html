Two teapots, one very premature victory lap

2026-10-27 · milestone lesson

'Cool, fibo seems to be working, let's start running fib's matrix in lig.'

The teapot had changed colour. Promotion time! Except this was permission to try a matrix, not evidence that a new model could be trusted with somebody's face.

The teapot's timesheet

Native BF16 smoke     Steps   CFG   Canvas       Execution time
FIBO 2.0 Dev             30     5   1024 x 1024    195.409 s
FIBO 2.0 Edit Turbo       4     1   1024 x 1024     91.046 s
Seed: 4242. Sampler/scheduler: Euler/simple.

Two saved, nonblank outputs. The edit changed the blue teapot to dark red and kept the broad composition, with small texture changes. Two useful observations. Not a quality department.

The pantry inspection came first

The handoff had five matching NAS registry entries out of ten pinned plan items. It also had an earlier zero-of-ten result whose exact origin was unknown. We kept that uncertainty, rather than inventing a story about the failed network address. Those were archive observations, not proof of a working pipeline. Source: the private FIBO readiness handoff dated 26 October.

The local smoke needed native ComfyUI support, the selected diffusion weights, the encoder and the exact VAE. File existence was one check; publisher-hash identity and server-visible loader choices were separate checks. The smoke packet records the local hashes and live schema. Non-commercial use was explicitly confirmed. Auxiliary licence and archive work remained open.

The two recipes came from native support PR 17240 and the negative-conditioning correction in PR 17285. Four-step editing was not borrowed as a fast Dev preset. That shortcut stayed outside the kitchen.

Alternatives, with the lid firmly on

  • Download every precision first? A smaller complete bundle answered the smoke question. Next!
  • Treat an API template as a local implementation? Different execution route. No.

What still hurts

A colour-change smoke establishes that one edit ran. It says nothing conclusive about anime, portrait identity or a whole comparison ladder. The next problem was less photogenic: getting the actual prompt into the actual node.
