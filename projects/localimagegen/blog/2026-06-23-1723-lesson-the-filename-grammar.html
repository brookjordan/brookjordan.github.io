Lesson — one filename grammar, and seeds in the name

2026-06-23 17:23 · lesson architecture

Filenames are the database in this project, so the grammar matters. It now lives in exactly one place: src/naming.js. Re-implementing it anywhere else is how drift and phantom data creep in.

The grammar:

<model>_<scene>_sNN[_t<dur>s]_<seed5>_.<ext>
e.g. krea2_van_s08_t12s_00042_.png
  • sNN = step count, _t<dur>s_ = render seconds, trailing 5-digit number = the seed.
  • The trailing number is the seed, not a counter. This was the big pivot. Early sweeps used ComfyUI's auto-increment suffix (_00001_, _00002_), which collided with the idea of "versions" and produced duplicate/garbage cells. We moved to seed-in-the-name (_00042_ for seed 42, _02026_ for seed 2026) and rewrote stamping to read the real seed from ComfyUI history rather than trusting the auto suffix.

Consequence: stamp.js moves raw ComfyUI outputs into masters/ under the stamped name (duration + seed); masters/ is canonical, and the ComfyUI output dir is just a drop zone. Anything that needs to know a cell's identity goes through naming.js — never a bespoke regex.
