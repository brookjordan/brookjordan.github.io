The step-count reckoning — who thrived, who made soup

2026-06-24 17:53 · results content

The original question was simple: how few steps can each model get away with? We render every model at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8… steps and look. The answer turned out to be a personality test, and the results surprised us.

Exhibit A — the same doll, 1 step vs 8

Chroma is the full undistilled model: no turbo shortcuts, so at a single step it has essentially nothing to work with.

1 step · 35s 2 steps · 47s 4 steps · 93s 8 steps · 183s

Same seed, same prompt. One step is a smear of pink; eight steps is a porcelain ball-jointed doll in a sunlit parlour — fur stole, teacups, the lot. That's the whole thesis in two frames.

Exhibit B — fast ≠ faithful

Now SDXL with a Lightning LoRA, 2 steps, 4 seconds:

Clean, warm, beautifully lit… and completely missing the doll the prompt asked for.

This is the trap. The turbo models are fast and pretty at 2 steps, but they'll happily paint a gorgeous empty room and forget the subject entirely. Speed bought coherence, not obedience.

Exhibit C — the worst quadrant: slow and wrong

The cruelest cell in the whole matrix. Chroma, "spirit" scene, 2 steps — and it still took 46 seconds to produce this:

Forty-six seconds. For abstract green.

Undistilled model + too few steps = you pay full price and get soup. You cannot guess this from a spec sheet. You have to see it — which is the entire reason the matrix exists.

The 2-step league table — one scene, fourteen models

Same "spirit" prompt, same 2 steps, sorted fastest → slowest:

juggernaut · 4s sdxlbase · 4s zimage · 15s flux9b · 25s
schnell · 26s qwen2512 · 35s chroma · 46s ideogram · 67s

What we actually learned, model by model:

  • juggernaut / sdxl (4s) — lightning fast, coherent, but collapse the busy scene down to one hero creature (a lone fuzzy critter) and quietly drop the rest of the cast.
  • flux9b (25s) & qwen2512 (35s) — the surprise overachievers: at a mere 2 steps they already place the tardigrade, the bird, the fuzzy gremlin and the deer spirit. Punching way above their step count.
  • chroma (46s) — green soup.
  • ideogram (67s) — a near-black void. The slowest model produced the worst image.

The takeaway we didn't expect

"How many steps?" is the wrong question. The right one is "how many steps for this model," and the honest answer ranges from 1 to 20+. That realisation is exactly why the config grew per-model step ladders instead of one global list — and why the gallery has a "compare by time budget" mode, so you can ask "best image in 30 seconds" and watch the rankings completely reshuffle.
