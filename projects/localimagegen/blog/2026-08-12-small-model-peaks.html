Eight small models, three to five gigabytes

2026-08-12 19:25 · data memory tooling apple-silicon

The low-memory SD1.x/SD2.x cohort finally got an intensive allocator sweep on the development Mac (M3 Max, 36 GB). Peaks land between 3.2 GB and 4.8 GB at their researched landscape canvases — well under SDXL's ~11 GB, and in the ballpark an 8 GB Mini might survive if OS + Turbo leave enough headroom.

The run

yarn mem:sweep:intensive:quiet \
  sd15 sd15lcm sdturbo dreamshaper8 realisticvision epicrealism dreamlike sd21

Five rounds × eight models, full ComfyUI restart between every measurement. Rich readings and the reconciled report live at:

  • renders/mem-archive/2026-08-12-small-models-intensive.json
  • renders/mem-archive/2026-08-12-small-models-report.json

(build/data/mem-*.json holds the same snapshot until the next intensive sweep overwrites it. The archive under renders/ is the durable copy.)

Every reading was full-load / unimodal. No partial-load mode showed up on this machine with this budget.

What this does not mean

  • Not gallery footprints. The fixed page bakes build/data/mem.json from the plain yarn mem:sweep (legacy free-RAM delta). Intensive report → gallery is still a missing bridge; run the plain sweep next and compare.
  • Not Mini admission. These peaks are from a quiet 36 GB laptop. The Mini still has to prove complete load, no swap, recovery, and Turbo health under 8 GB unified memory.
  • Legacy psutil still lies. On several models it over-counted by 19–61% vs the allocator peak — same lesson as the actual numbers.

Next: plain mem:sweep for gallery mem.json, then Mini admission for whoever wins the quality-vs-peak trade.
