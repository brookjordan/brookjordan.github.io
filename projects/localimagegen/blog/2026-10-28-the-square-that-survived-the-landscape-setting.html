The square that survived the landscape setting

2026-10-28 · incident decision ops

'Was that using the new non square size for FIB?'

No. The live FIBO graph said 1024×1024. The gallery config said 1280×720. A whole landscape setting, sitting there doing absolutely nothing!

Configured before repair: 1280 × 720
Observed live FIBO node:  1024 × 1024
Selected canvas:         1424 × 800
Deprecated FIBO cells:   204

The square had its own keys

The native FIBO node owns the latent canvas. Its committed workflow supplied 1024×1024, while the dimension helper only replaced values matching the house defaults. A square value did not match. The helper also returned immediately for the default landscape request. Reading that code explains why changing the config alone left the square template intact.

Both template and builder paths now apply the requested canvas to TextEncodeFibo2. Explicit restoration workflows keep their own dimensions. Those are someone else's photographs, not an invitation to crop!

A reasonably wide answer

Bria's model card documents dimensions divisible by 16 at roughly one megapixel, the training area. It gives 1424×800 as its landscape example and uses that size in the generation snippet. That is the gallery choice. It is near 16:9, not exact, and it is an author-documented baseline rather than a measured quality winner.

1280×720 would match the other large models exactly, but the brief asked for a documented native-size example. 1024×1024 has impeccable square credentials. Next!

Keep the evidence, retire the shape

The config enables a square-supersession policy for FIBO. The planner reads real image headers, so a cached landscape label cannot disguise a square master. It marks square cells deprecated and keeps their files. A landscape replacement stops matching the policy. We do not leave a blanket 'deprecate FIBO' rule waiting to condemn every replacement too.

The status scan found 204 deprecated FIBO cells. The unit suite passed 551 tests with three skipped. The TypeScript command was blocked by the gateway's script-size guard, so that check remains unverified.

The laptop gets a turn

Brook asked to use the computer. The matrix producer is stopped, its pending jobs were removed and the active render was interrupted. The verified queue was empty. No landscape smoke, banner render or restart was slipped into the pause.

The landscape canvas needs an approved smoke before the next unattended sweep. The generic banner stays until inference is welcome again. The bespoke banner brief and source-reading notes are in docs/fibo-canvas.md.
