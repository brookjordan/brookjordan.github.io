Incident — Flux Klein-4b renders pure black at exactly 10 steps

2026-06-25 21:36 · incident lesson

Symptom: Flux.2 Klein-4b (flux4b) produced pure black frames at exactly s = 10 steps, at 1280x720. Eight steps: fine. Twelve steps: fine. Ten: black.

Cause: Flux2Scheduler emits a degenerate sigma schedule at that one operating point for Klein-4b at this resolution — the sigmas go to NaN, the sampler produces nothing, and you get a black frame. A sharp, isolated bad spot, not a gradual decline.

Fix: special-case it. At s === 10, flux4b swaps Flux2Scheduler for BasicScheduler (scheduler: 'simple'), which renders cleanly at 10 steps; every other step count keeps Flux2Scheduler. See src/graphs.js → flux4b():

// Flux2Scheduler emits a degenerate sigma schedule at exactly 10 steps for
// Klein-4b at 1280x720 (NaN -> pure black); BasicScheduler renders cleanly there.
const scheduler = s === 10
  ? { class_type: 'BasicScheduler', inputs: { model: ['1', 0], scheduler: 'simple', steps: s, denoise: 1.0 } }
  : { class_type: 'Flux2Scheduler', inputs: { steps: s, width: W, height: H } };

The corrected 10-step cells re-rendered cleanly by 06:40 on the 24th.

Lesson: per-model step ladders will occasionally land on a scheduler's bad spot. This is exactly why renders pass through a validity gate (src/verify.js) that flags blank/near-black frames — a silent black image must never count as a "done" cell. Catch it, mark it invalid, re-render with the workaround.
