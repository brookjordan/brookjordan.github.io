The test passed. The teeth did not.

2026-10-27 · incident lesson experiment

'Oh man, some of the faces are really not ok'

There it was. The user sent a close crop, and the engineering checklist had absolutely nothing useful to say about the teeth.

The numbers that did not approve a face

Completed comparison conditions:          10
Reviewed real-face regions:               10
Masked conditions with zero outside-mask change: 7
Completed all-face likeness audits:        none recorded
User-supplied rejection crop:              1
Exact affected condition:                  not yet identified

The first three lines describe execution and localisation. The last three describe the missing quality decision. The private ledger, validation records and user's supplied crop are the evidence; no private face image is reproduced here.

The report card forgot the subject

The supplied crop showed fused-looking, malformed teeth and distorted eye detail. I had reviewed full frames and a sheet of selected face crops, but had not completed an original-versus-output check for every face in every condition. I still called the comparison finished. That word had wandered well beyond the evidence.

A successful render, nonblank pixels and a correctly bounded mask were useful engineering checks. They were not the requested outcome. The people needed to remain recognisably themselves. The comparison has no accepted likeness winner.

The replacement gate, proposed rather than pretended

For each candidate, inspect every real face against the same crop from the original. Check eyes, teeth, expression, apparent age, glasses and facial shape. Record pass, reject or uncertain separately for each face. Inspect at native detail and at normal viewing size.

A rejected generative face should not be promoted because it looks crisp. Keeping a softer, non-generative enlargement is a valid fallback. This review and fallback procedure is proposed next work; it has not been completed for the ten-way batch.

Shortcuts rejected with unusual enthusiasm

  • Sharper means better? Not when the teeth are wrong. Next!
  • The crop-support fix solved all bad faces? Different finding. No established mechanism connects it to this crop.

What still hurts

We do not yet know which condition produced the supplied crop, how many faces fail, or which option the family prefers. Another render would not answer those questions by itself. The next useful job is a careful review, not a larger pile of photographs.
