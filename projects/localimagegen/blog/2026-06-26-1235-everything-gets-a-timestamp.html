What time did that happen? (Everything gets a timestamp)

2026-06-26 12:35 · decision

Debugging the overnight loop, I kept hitting the same wall: "okay, but when did that happen?" — and half the records just shrugged. A master on disk, a seed in seeds.auto.json, a cell in meta.json: plenty of what, almost no when. So this one's less a feature and more a law.

The law

Any event, log line, notification, or persisted state record carries an ISO-8601 timestamp. And when building anything new, the standing question becomes: "where do I record when this happened?" If there's a reasonable place, it gets one.

The fills

The audit turned up a few gaps to close:

  • A shared nowIso() in src/util.ts, killing the duplicated new Date().toISOString() calls scattered through src/config.ts. One helper, one format, no drift.
  • renderedAt on every CellMeta — pulled from ComfyUI's execution_success message when we have it, falling back to the master file's mtime so even old renders get a best-effort when instead of a null.
  • A recorded time when the autopilot adds a seed. seeds.auto.json stays a plain number[] (don't break the effective-seeds union), and the "when" lives in the event log from the resilience plan.

What deliberately stays un-stamped

Filenames. They keep the render duration (t<dur>s), not a wall-clock — the one filename grammar stays stable and content-derived, so a re-render doesn't churn the name. The "when" belongs in meta.json and the logs, never baked into the identity of the file.

Why a whole plan for something this small?

Because it's a convention, not a one-off — cross-cutting by nature, and the only way a convention survives contact with future-me is to write it down (it's going into CLAUDE.md). It's cheap insurance: the five minutes now versus the hour, some night months from now, squinting at an undated record and swearing.
