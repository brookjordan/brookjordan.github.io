The actual numbers: how much memory does each model use?

2026-07-06 17:00 · tooling data memory models

After building the allocator-accurate measurement pipeline — /lig/mem custom node, phase tracking via WebSocket, reconcile logic — we ran the intensive sweep. Here are the real numbers for an M3 Max with 36 GB unified memory.

The methodology

Each measurement restarts ComfyUI from scratch, calls POST /free to clear the allocator, records the driver_allocated_memory() baseline (should be ≈ 0 GB), then runs a full text-to-image render and tracks the driver allocation at 200ms intervals throughout. The peak is the highest value seen. Weights is the driver value at the sample phase boundary (all loaders done, KSampler about to start). Activations is peak minus weights.

The three SDXL models below are from a 5-round reconciled sweep (5 × 3 = 15 measurements, median across all rounds). The broader table is from single-round snapshots with one measurement per model.

The psutil legacy values range from −16% to +43% of the real peak. The old metric didn't just overcount consistently — it could go either direction depending on page-cache state.

What this tells us

SDXL uses ~11 GB peak, not 6–7 GB. The on-disk size (6–7 GB for a SafeTensors file) is a common shorthand but it's not what the Metal driver holds. The loaded model expands in memory, and denoising activations are roughly as large as the weights themselves.

Quantized models don't save as much as you'd think. flux4b (Flux at 4-bit NF4 via GGUF) has a 15 GB file but peaks at 10.4 GB — the quantization reduces the weights but activations during denoising are still full precision. schnell at fp8 peaks at 11.7 GB.

Large multimodal models demand large headroom. ovis, zimage, and qwen2512 peak at 15–18 GB. On a 36 GB machine that leaves 18–21 GB for the OS and other processes, which is fine. On a 24 GB machine, those models wouldn't load completely.

The psutil divergence is unpredictable. The old metric was sometimes close (illustrious: +8%) and sometimes absurd (pony: +43%). There's no safe correction factor. The only reliable number is the allocator peak.
