Every model renders the house canvas

2026-10-27 · decision lesson

'it doesn't seem like a fair test otherwise'

The operator's verdict on FIBO's row in the gallery, and it is the whole argument in one line. The matrix exists to compare models on the same scene, at the same seed, doing the same job. FIBO was doing the same job on a different canvas, and the comparison was quietly unfair from its first render.

Who renders what

House canvas (the engine default):  1280 x 720
  chroma, juggernaut, illustrious, pony, realvisxl and friends
Mini-tier overrides (deliberate, low-memory):
  704 x 384   sd15, sd15lcm, sd21
  640 x 384   sdturbo, realisticvision, epicrealism
  768 x 432   dreamshaper8, dreamlike
The odd one out:                    fibo, 1024 x 1024

Square masters superseded:          287
Square renders cancelled in flight: 51

Nine models pin a small canvas on purpose: they are the Mac Mini tier, admitted at reduced resolutions so they cost less memory. Everybody else inherits the default. FIBO was the only model that overrode the default at all, and it overrode it the wrong way.

How a square slipped in

FIBO's architecture hands the canvas to its text encoder: TextEncodeFibo2 carries width, height and prompt together, so the shape travels inside the conditioning instead of living in a separate latent node. The native ComfyUI template ships square, our first smoke ran square, and the config row copied the smoke. Every other model inherits the house default precisely because nobody typed a canvas for them.

The restoration run had already shown FIBO does rectangles happily: the photograph was edited at roughly 1280 by 1056, its own aspect, and the shape arrived intact. Square was not a limit. It was an inheritance nobody had questioned.

The fix: inherit, then let validation do the sweeping

The config row now omits the override and inherits 1280 by 720, with a comment explaining why. The 287 square masters are superseded by the engine's own invalidation: run --verify deep-checks every master against its model's configured canvas, files the squares as wrong-dims, re-renders them wide, and the new masters validate clean. The tier settles; nothing re-renders again.

What the fix deliberately does not use is lig deprecate model=fibo. A deprecation selector matches forever: every future run would classify every FIBO cell deprecated and re-render it again, and again, for as long as the repo exists. The illustrious step deprecations survive in config as honest tombstones for rungs nobody wants, not as a re-render queue. Wrong canvas is not a tombstone; it is a stale byte, and stale bytes are what the verify tier is for.

The square files stay on disk, cell by cell, until their wide replacements exist and validate. Nothing is deleted ahead of its successor.

Alternatives, measured against the door

  • Crop the squares to 16:9? Discards a quarter of the frame the siblings keep. Next!
  • Keep square as a permanent FIBO category? A side-by-side where every frame needs its own letterbox. Next!
  • Upscale the squares outward? An aspect lie, with interpolation. Next!

What still hurts

Two hundred and eighty-seven renders, most of a GPU-day, spent learning a rule that is now the absence of one config line. FIBO's wide quality is unjudged until the re-renders land: we have only ever judged this model square (the smoke) and at a photograph's aspect (the restoration). The matrix will now tell us the third thing, and the convention is written where the next model will read it before its first render.
