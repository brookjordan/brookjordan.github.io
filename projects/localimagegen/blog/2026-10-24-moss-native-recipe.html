MOSS gets a native recipe, not the global bootstrap

2026-10-24 · decision content

The first 50-step MOSS image came back all black. A perfect, flawless, lightless void of an image, delivered with a straight face and a green tick in ComfyUI's history. If you have ever wanted a diffusion model to hand you a rip-off of Malevich with no explanation, MOSS-Image Base at 50 steps is your guy. This post is the story of how we got there, why it was our fault twice over, and how the model finally earned its place in the matrix.

Built in the dark (on purpose)

MOSS-Image Base and SFT got separate matrix entries, mossbase and mosssft, while neither had rendered a single pixel on this Mac. The running ComfyUI did not even know the MOSS encoder type and the weight files were nowhere on disk. Cheeky? Entirely. Safe? Also entirely, because nothing was submitted and the running gallery sweep was not touched.

The research note compares the authors' guidance with the faster ComfyUI templates, and the authors win: Euler, CFG 3 and negative-branch layer-22 skipping for both releases, with fixed 10/25/50-step comparisons. The 50-step setting follows the authors' baseline. The lower rungs are drafts, not a claimed quality plateau. INT8/W4A8 loaders and the small FLUX.2 VAE still need MPS qualification. Sources: the author guide, the SFT card and src/graphs.ts.

Prompting turned out to be a whole personality. MOSS accepts descriptive prose, tags and experimental JSON layouts, and the initial matrix uses its English prose scenes. Tags and layout JSON pass through the graph unchanged too, with regression tests covering all three inputs. One rule was learned the hard way and written down: a generated caption is not proof that an edit or layout instruction worked. Sources: the official prompt skill and test/graphs.moss.test.ts.

And then our own planner tried to "help". Setting ceiling activates its dynamic low-step bootstrap even when the model carries an explicit step array, which is exactly the kind of eager assistance you do not want. MOSS therefore takes the existing fixed-ladder route without that field, and no other model's scheduling changed. Sources: src/config.ts, src/reconcile.ts and the MOSS planning regression test.

Offline verification: 507 tests passed, three skipped; the fast type/path/index gate and the dynamic gallery build passed. Live schema checks caught the missing files and encoder type before submission. No second controller was started.

Activation, and the black square strikes

Brook authorised installation and replacement of the running matrix. Four pinned weight files passed SHA-256 checks, ComfyUI went to v0.42.1 with PyTorch 2.12.1 retained, and the bot completed jobs after the idle-boundary restart. Then the fun started. The first adaptation had kept simple scheduling and automatic BF16 VAE precision, and the authors' ComfyUI guide says sgm_uniform and FP32 VAE, so both the builder and the materialised graphs were corrected. The installed KJNodes loader supplies model-scoped VAE precision.

Still black. An author-aligned retry: black again, with a successful history entry both times. So we opened up the saved pre-decode latent and counted: zero finite values out of 460,800 elements. Not "dim". Not "dark". Not-a-single-finite-number, before the VAE ever ran. That locates the disagreement upstream of decoding without pinning the first faulty operation, and it is the difference between "the renderer is broken" and "the sampling is producing garbage we then faithfully decode". Base 50 is excluded from automatic work; SFT 50 stays unqualified; the matrix runs 10/25-step drafts, which is a statement of what is proven, not a claim about the authors' baseline. The black images and the sampled latent are preserved with hashes under data/runs/2026-10-24-moss-inclusion/, because a mystery you cannot reproduce is just a story.

The stamp path now validates pixels before importing a master and keeps the rejected raw output and any prior valid master. A regression reproduced the earlier false-success case and passed with the guard; the actual black retry was rejected too. Sources: src/stamp.ts, src/reconcile.ts and test/stamp.validation.test.ts. No more free black squares.

Qualified, finally

Both variants completed normal gallery qualification at 10 and 25 steps, and the 25-step van images were inspected like suspicious customs declarations. A single persistent full-matrix controller, PID 1565, then started with --priority-models mossbase,mosssft. Its client 5593f3b3-bc4c-47fb-8e6b-7060a87c2ffc completed two further Base van images, at seeds 2026 and 1670940986, and both masters passed an independent pixel/structure check. This is live inclusion evidence, not a smoke test wearing a lab coat. Sources: base-native-draft-qualification.json, sft-native-draft-qualification.json and matrix-evidence.json in the run directory.

What still hurts

The rest of the matrix keeps rendering, and the 50-step numerical cause is still out there: somewhere between layer 22 and the sampler, Base produces an all-nonfinite latent at 50 steps and nobody can yet point at the first operation that goes bad. Two rungs of the ladder are roped off with a "do not feed" sign, and the sign stays up until someone finds the culprit.
